# Project Brief - Eye Care Management System

## Overview
The Eye Care Management System is a modern web platform designed to streamline operations for specialized eye care clinics and hospitals. It digitizes the patient journey from service discovery and appointment booking to clinical consultation, diagnosis, optical prescriptions, and follow-ups.

## Target Users
- **Patient**: Seeks eye care services, books appointments, views prescriptions, and tracks medical history.
- **Doctor (Ophthalmologist/Optometrist)**: Manages patient queues, performs consultations, records eye examinations, diagnoses conditions, and writes prescriptions.
- **Hospital Admin**: Oversees hospital operations, staff schedules, appointment slots, department services, and analytics.

## Core Objectives
1. Provide separate, role-tailored web portals for Patients, Doctors, and Hospital Admins.
2. Maintain structured, digital medical records for eye care consultations.
3. Streamline appointment scheduling and prevent slot conflicts.
4. Deliver a high-performance, accessible, and responsive user interface.
